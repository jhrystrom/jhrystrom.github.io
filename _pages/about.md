My research is about building the technical tools to _evaluate_ and _use_ "AI"-systems to build a more equitable and competent public sector - rather than degrade state capacity. 

"AI" can mean many things ([or at least 5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31755), as we argue here), but I'm most excited (and worried) about agentic systems, which [I don't think we evaluate well currently](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.20617).

I'm also interested in socio-technical evaluations of other systems like [measuring the discrepancy in how humans vs text embeddings represent tricky policy problems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27168)

When I'm not researching, I like to play jazz ukulele (or [live karaoke](https://karaokecollective.com/)), read books, and chit chat about big and small.
