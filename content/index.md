I build the technical tools to *evaluate* and *use* "AI" systems, so they make the public
sector more equitable and competent rather than degrading state capacity. I'm a DPhil student
at the [Oxford Internet Institute](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/), and I've taught on the MSc in
Social Data Science.

"AI" can mean many things ([or at least 5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31755), as we argue
here), but I'm most excited — and most worried — about agentic systems, which
[I don't think we evaluate well](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.20617). I also work on
sociotechnical evaluation more broadly, such as
[measuring the gap between how humans and text embeddings represent tricky policy problems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27168). My most up-to-date research publications can be found on [google scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=bKLjkOMAAAAJ&hl=en).

When I'm not researching, I play jazz ukulele (and [live karaoke](https://karaokecollective.com/)),
read books, and chat about things big and small.
